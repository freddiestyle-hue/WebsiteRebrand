01 · Crawlability

Search engines can find the site. Whether AI engines can read it cleanly is the question.

Sitemap reachable, robots permissive, canonicals self-referencing. One redirect chain to clear.

/sitemap.xml resolves at the root and lists 127 URLs. We walked them all. None 404. Twelve redirect to current URLs. One chain in /old-blog hops three times before landing — agents drop the trail after the second hop.

Robots is permissive across the board. GPTBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot all crawl without restriction. The right posture for a business that wants to be cited.

02 · Structured data

Organization is present. Article and FAQPage are missing on the very pages that needed them.

Three JSON-LD blocks across the site. Two are useful. The third is on the wrong page.

The homepage emits an Organization block with name and url. It does not have logo, sameAs, or contactPoint. Those three fields are how an AI agent links your domain to the entity it has indexed elsewhere — your Facebook page, Google Business profile, YouTube. Without them, the agent sees a domain with a name but no trail of evidence.

Your blog has a real FAQ section at the bottom of every long-form post. Eight posts in the May archive have a "Frequently asked" block. ChatGPT cannot tell. The page emits the words but not the structured data that says these are questions and these are answers.

{ "@context": "https://schema.org", "@type": "Organization", "name": "Boss Fuel Trailers", "url": "https://bossfueltrailers.com" // missing: logo, sameAs, contactPoint }

03 · AEO surfaces

There is no llms.txt. AI agents hitting your domain bounce off a 404.

The structured-data layer is for machines that speak schema. The AEO layer is for the new generation of agents that need a plaintext map.

/llms.txt is the convention that locked in across Anthropic, Perplexity, and OpenAI in early 2026. A plain markdown file at the root telling an agent what your site is, what the canonical answers are, and where to find them. Most SMB sites still 404 here. So does yours.

# Boss Fuel Trailers > Aluminum DOT-compliant fuel trailers for fleets, ranches, construction. > Built in Texas. Shipped in 4–6 weeks. ## Canonical answers - [Why aluminum, not steel](/learn/why-aluminum-fuel-trailers) - [DOT compliance checklist](/learn/dot-compliance) - [Sizing your trailer](/learn/sizing) ## Specs - [14ft aluminum spec sheet](/specs/14ft.pdf) - [20ft aluminum spec sheet](/specs/20ft.pdf) ## Contact sales@bossfueltrailers.com · +1-555-0140
F.03.1
Critical

Ship /llms.txt. Fifteen minutes. One file.

The next time an agent crawls you, it has a map instead of a maze. Update the file when you ship a new pillar post.
