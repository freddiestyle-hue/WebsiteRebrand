Design system / 02 Identity & Marks
Section 02 · The signature

Wordmark, B1, one dot between them.

The wordmark is the primary signature. The rivet B1 is the favicon and small-scale fallback. The dot is sacred. Below 14px the wordmark is replaced by the B1.

02 · 1 Wordmark

Inter Tight 700, −0.05em, dot at 0.32em.

The wordmark is set in Inter Tight 700, tight-tracked. The dot is rendered as a perfect circle in --accent, sized at 0.32× the cap-height, with a 0.04em vertical nudge to sit on the baseline ink. Its position is fixed at cx=288 cy=58 r=9 in the source SVG and is never adjusted independently of the type.

Below 14px the wordmark is replaced by the B1 mark — character spacing collapses and the dot loses fidelity. Maximum size has no cap, but above 144px the dot diameter must be hand-checked.

Clear space

Minimum clear space on every side is the height of the lowercase e — i.e., 0.5em. Nothing crosses this margin. Stamp-style mono labels and arrow callouts may sit at the boundary if they read as a separate cluster.

02 · 2 Rivet B1

The square, the rivet, the dot.

Rivet B1 is the small-scale identity. It carries an internal R-stroke and a green dot at the 62/60 grid position. Used as favicon, app icon, social avatar, and anywhere the wordmark cannot read.

Inverse · paper bg

Geometry · the source numbers

  • Container: 80×80 with a 6-px stroke. Always square. No padding inside the container — the stroke kisses an 8px boundary.
  • Stroke: square caps, mitre joins. Stroke-width 6. Path: M22 60 V20 H44 a12 12 0 0 1 12 12 v0 a12 12 0 0 1 -12 12 H30 l16 16
  • Dot: cx=62 cy=60 r=6. Fill #8FBF3F on dark, #4A6E18 on light.
  • Locked. The path, the dot position, and the radius do not change between releases.

02 · 3 The dot

The dot is sacred. It has rules.

One green dot per page in the wordmark. The 8px stamp dot before mono labels is the only allowed echo. Every other use is a misuse.

  • Always rendered in --accent (#8FBF3F), never the deep variant.
  • Always trailing the wordmark, never leading.
  • Always a perfect circle. Never replaced with a bullet, en-dot, middot character, or square.
  • Never animated, stroked, gradiented, or outlined.
  • Never sized below 8px (it stops being a dot and becomes a fleck).
  • Never appears alone in a layout. The dot is paired — with a wordmark, with a stamp, with a label.

02 · 4 Social

Open Graph cards, two layouts only.

1200×630 cards. Hero kind for the homepage and big essays. Stamp kind for everything else (Field Notes, MRI report, diagnostic). No images, no photos, no decoration. Type, dot, URL.

Card rules

  • 1200×630 export. SVG source, PNG flatten with subpixel AA.
  • Wordmark top-left at 22px equivalent. Stamp top-right.
  • Headline bottom-left at 48–64px display, balanced wrap, italic on the second clause.
  • URL bottom-left under headline at 11px mono. No https:// prefix.
  • Page title and OG title may differ. The OG title is shorter and sharper.
02 · 5 Email

One signature. Plain text, no images.

No logo image. No banner. No "Sent from my…" footer. The email signature is a four-line block in plain text — name, role, URL, line. Inter Tight or system stack.

Rules
