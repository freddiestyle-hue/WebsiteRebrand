Design system/04 Patterns
Section 04 · Composed blocks

The page writes itself.

Long-form pages are assembled from a small kit of composed blocks. Hero, section head, TLDR, pull quote, numbox, migration callout, audience triad, dark closer.

04 · 1Hero

Stamp, headline, dek. Three lines.

The hero is text-only. Stamp at top in mono. Display heading with one italic clause. Italic serif dek beneath capped at 700px. No image, no shape, no scroll-cue.

Hero · primary
A senior growth practice · Cape Town & remote

Growth infrastructure for operators.

Two-week diagnostic. Six-week build. Twelve-week run. Fortnightly read-out. Specific numbers, not a deck.

04 · 2Section heads

Numbered, 2-px ink rule above.

Every long-form section opens with a 240/1fr split. Mono label and 56px numeral on the left, 44px headline on the right. The 2-pixel ink rule above the row carries the whole composition.

Module02

Build. Stack, instrumentation, ten experiments.

04 · 3TLDR

One paragraph. Stamp on the left.

The TLDR is the first block on every long-form page. 90px stamp column, 1fr paragraph. Inter Tight 17 medium, ink-pretty wrap. Never more than three sentences.

TL · DR

Four founders. Same growth question. The honest answer is rarely the loud one — and it's almost always cheaper than the playbook says.

04 · 4Pull quote

Italic serif, two ink rules above and below.

The pull quote is the only place a whole sentence is set in italic. 32px Newsreader, 1.25 line height. Cite on a new line in mono.

"The work isn't to ship more. It's to ship the right thing, instrumented, on the right cadence — and to know within a fortnight whether it's bending the curve."
— field note 04 · Cadence over volume
04 · 5Numbox

Sage background, 3-pixel deep-green rail.

The numbox is where data lands inside prose. Sage tint. 3-pixel accent-deep rail on the left. Two or four columns. Mono key in accent-deep above a 32px Inter Tight 700 value with an italic serif unit.

04 · 6Migration

Ink callout, old strikethrough, new in accent.

The migration callout marks any version-bump in the system or any deprecated value on a page. Ink background. Mono diff body in two states — --on-ink-faint with strikethrough for old, --accent for new.

v0.2 → v1.0

Two values move. Everything else is additive.

--accent: #6FB582 → #8FBF3F
--accent-deep: #3F7553 → #4A6E18
04 · 7Audience triad

Three columns, hairline divider, no fill.

Used on the homepage to address three audiences in one gesture. Each column is a stamp + headline + lede. No icons, no buttons, no backgrounds. Three is the count.

For founders

Numbers are live, next move unclear.

You have revenue and a team. You are not sure where the next 90 days of effort should land. The diagnostic.

For operators

You inherited a stack.

Marketing exists, sales exists, the data exists somewhere. Senior eyes, instrumentation, and a fortnightly read-out.

For boards

You need a sober read.

The portfolio company is shipping but the curve is flat. A two-week independent diagnostic, written for a board pack.

04 · 8Dark closer

Ink end-CTA, used once per page.

Dark surfaces are end-of-page furniture. The end-CTA is the only mandatory dark surface, and it is used at most once. Headline left at 1.4fr, supporting block right at 1fr.

If your numbers are live but the next move is unclear, that's the diagnostic.

Two-week engagement

Senior eyes on your funnel, your spend, and the next 90 days. £4k. No retainer required to start.

04 · 9Footer

Mono everywhere. Wordmark, three columns, line.

The site footer pairs the wordmark with three mono columns: The work, Field notes, Contact. No sub-stamps, no logos, no social rails. Bottom line is "v1.0 · 2026".
