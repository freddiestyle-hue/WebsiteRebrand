The system, in twelve sections.

This is the canonical reference for everything that ships under the rivett mark. Tokens, type, marks, components, voice. If it isn't here, it isn't part of the system. If it conflicts with what you see live, the system wins; the page is wrong.

Section 01 Colour

Paper, ink, a single green.

The palette is small on purpose. Two warm papers, three inks, one accent in two values. No tertiaries, no semantic ramps, no gradient stops. If the design needs another colour, the design is doing too much.

Section 02 Type

Three families, three jobs.

Inter Tight does the work. Newsreader italic does the whisper. DM Mono does the labelling. No fourth family, no decorative cuts, no display web fonts beyond these three.

Section 03 Spacing

4-pixel base, named scale.

No raw pixel values in component CSS. Everything routes through a token. The scale is geometric enough to be predictable and tight enough that you can hold it in your head.

Radii

Default is zero. Rivett is square. The only allowed radii are --r-sm: 2px for very small chrome and --r-pill: 999px for the dot mark. No 4px, no 6px, no 8px corners.

Section 04 Marks

Wordmark, B1, one dot between them.

The wordmark is the primary signature. The rivet B1 is the favicon and small-scale fallback. The dot is sacred. Position is fixed at cx=288 cy=58 r=9 in the source SVG and is never adjusted independently of the type.

Wordmark · primary rivett
Rivet B1 · favicon & ≤14px

The dot — rules

  • Always rendered in --accent (#8FBF3F), never the deep variant.
  • Always trailing the wordmark, never leading.
  • Always a perfect circle. Never replaced with a bullet, en-dot, or middot character.
  • One per page in the wordmark. The 8px stamp dot before mono labels is the only allowed echo.
  • Never animated, stroked, or gradiented.

Section 10 Voice

A senior operator, writing late at night.

Not a marketer. Short declarative sentences. Specific numbers. Concrete verbs. The em-dash is replaced by the period. Two short sentences beat one long one every single time.

· Do

  • One idea per sentence.
  • Specific numbers — $10M, 30 markets, 40–60 min.
  • Concrete verbs: run, ship, instrument, decline.
  • Quoted italic for the emotional beat.
  • En-dash for ranges only: 40–60.
  • Question marks in the FAQ, generously.

· Don't

  • No emoji. Not in copy, headers, or CTAs.
  • No exclamation marks except inside a quote.
  • No em-dashes. Use the period.
  • No leverage, unlock, empower, 10x.
  • No question marks in headlines outside the FAQ.
  • No whole-sentence italic. Italic is the turn, not the statement.

The italic rule

Newsreader italic is the brand's whisper. It carries the second clause of a thought — never the first. Pair with a roman first clause. Like this. Always --ink-dim on light, --on-ink-italic on dark. Never --ink. Never bold + italic.

Section 11 Motion & focus

Quiet transitions, visible focus.

The brand barely animates. Hover crossfades the colour. Expanders rotate the marker. Page-level motion is reserved for the very rare full-bleed hero. Three durations, one easing curve. Reduced-motion is honoured everywhere.

Hover · colour --dur-1 120ms
Link colour, button bg, border swap.
Expand · transform --dur-2 200ms
FAQ marker rotation, TOC slide, modal open.
Page · transition --dur-3 320ms
Reserved. Almost never used.

Easing

--ease: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.6, 0.2, 1) — quick start, soft settle. One curve covers everything.

Focus ring

Two-pixel outline in --accent-deep on light surfaces, --accent on dark. Always offset 2px. Never removed. :focus-visible only — not :focus.
