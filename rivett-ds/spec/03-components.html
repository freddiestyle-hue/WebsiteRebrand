Design system/03 Components
Section 03 · The primitive library

Components. Fifteen, no more.

Every UI primitive in the system. Three buttons, six form controls, one card, one table, one FAQ, one modal, one banner, plus tag, tabs, pagination, breadcrumbs, toast. Compose, don't fork.

03 · 1 Buttons

Three buttons. Never four.

Primary is solid ink with an accent-deep hover. Secondary is the outline. Ghost is the text-only crumb with the trailing arrow. Inverse is what each becomes on dark. Two sizes — default and lg.

Anatomy

  • Padding: 11/18 default, 18/26 lg, 7/12 sm.
  • Type: --mono 11px, 0.22em tracked, uppercase.
  • Border: 1px ink. Never coloured. Never thicker.
  • Hover: 120ms colour swap to ink fill (or accent-deep on primary).
  • Trailing arrow → only for forward intent. Never on declines or destructive actions.
  • Disabled: 0.4 opacity, no events, copy may swap to a status string ("Awaiting reply").
03 · 2 Forms

Six controls. Square, hairline, ink.

Inputs are 1-pixel hairline ink-faint until hover. On focus the bottom edge becomes a 2-pixel accent-deep underscore. The label is mono uppercase above. Errors are mono red below.

03 · 3 Cards

One card. Stamp, headline, lede, foot.

There is one card. Module card, blog card, audience card, case study card — same component, different copy. Border hairline, no shadow, no radius. The hover state lifts the border to ink and tints the surface to paper-2.

03 · 4 Tables

Hairline rows, tabular numbers, mono head.

Tables read like a spec sheet. Mono uppercase header on paper-3. Body is Inter Tight 14.5. Numerics right-align with tabular figures. Hover swaps row to paper-2. No alternating-row stripes. No vertical dividers.

03 · 5 FAQ

Plus rotates to ×, 200ms.

The FAQ accordion is the only allowed expand-collapse component. Plus marker rotates 45° to a cross. Question is Inter Tight 600 19px; answer is 16/1.65 in ink-dim. Always numbered.

Q · 01What does a diagnostic actually look like?+

Two weeks. Two senior calls. A document at the end. Specific numbers, specific actions, the next ninety days. £4k. No retainer required to start.

Q · 02Do you only work with venture-backed companies?+

No. The work is the same — bootstrapped, sponsored, or backed. The only filter is whether the numbers are live and whether the team can absorb a fortnightly read-out.

Q · 03Will you sign an NDA before the diagnostic?+

Yes. The NDA is mutual and pre-printed. It takes ninety seconds.

03 · 6 Tabs

Mono labels, 2-pixel rule under the active.

Tabs are flat. No pill. No background fill. The active tab carries a 2-pixel ink rule on its bottom edge, sharing the hairline with the bar.

03 · 7 Tags · banners · toasts

Three signal surfaces, used sparingly.

Tag is for taxonomy. Banner is for page-level context. Toast is for transient confirmation. Toasts auto-dismiss in 4 seconds and never stack.

03 · 8 Modal

One modal pattern. Centre, paper, hairline.

Used for the diagnostic intake confirmation and for full-bleed image viewers. 480px max width. 32px padding. The modal is the only component allowed an elevation shadow (--shadow-2).
