Design system/06 Voice
Section 06 · Words on the page

A senior operator, writing late at night.

Not a marketer. Not a coach. A practitioner who has done the work, has the numbers, and is telling you the cheapest way to bend the curve. The em-dash is replaced by the period.

06 · 1Rules

Do. Don't.

The voice has been distilled into eighteen rules — nine to follow, nine to refuse. Read them once. Pin them to the wall. The system enforces about half automatically; the other half is on you.

· Do

  • One idea per sentence.
  • Specific numbers — £4k, 14 days, fortnightly.
  • Concrete verbs — run, ship, instrument, decline, halt.
  • Use the period where you wanted an em-dash.
  • Italic for the second clause. Never the first.
  • En-dash for ranges only — 40–60, D14–D30.
  • Question marks in the FAQ. Generously.
  • Lowercase the wordmark — rivett, never Rivett or RIVETT.
  • Sign off with initials and a date — — TR · 2025·09.

· Don't

  • No emoji. Not in copy, not in headers, not in CTAs.
  • No exclamation marks except inside a quote.
  • No em-dashes. Use the period.
  • No leverage, unlock, empower, 10x, synergy.
  • No journey, solution, partner with, at scale.
  • No question marks in headlines outside the FAQ.
  • No whole-sentence italic. Italic is the turn, not the statement.
  • No "we." Singular. The practice is one operator.
  • No future tense in pricing — "is", not "will be".

06 · 5Format conventions

Numbers, dates, currency, in one form.

A house style for the small things. The system reads as a single voice because the small things never vary.

Currency£4k · £12k / mo · £18,000 on board packs only.
Dates2025·09·14 in metadata · 2025·09 in prose · 2025·MM as placeholder.
Time spans02 wks · 04–06 wks · 14 days. Numerals leading; serif italic for the unit only in numbox.
NumeralsLead-zero pad versions, modules, vitals — v1.0, M · 01, V · 09.
ListsTitle-case the brand list (modules, audiences). Sentence-case all body lists.
Punctuation in monoUse · as separator inside mono labels — NOTE · 04 · CADENCE.
QuotationCurly quotes in prose “ ”. Straight quotes in mono " ". Single quotes only inside doubles.
06 · 6In the wild

Three sentences that pass the test.

If you can write a sentence that fits next to these without a flinch, you have the voice.

"The work isn't to ship more. It's to ship the right thing, instrumented, on the right cadence — and to know within a fortnight whether it's bending the curve."

"Half the value of a senior operator is the meeting that doesn't happen. The vendor that doesn't get hired."
— field note 03 · The decline letter
"A red is a finding. An insufficiency is a question that has not been asked."
