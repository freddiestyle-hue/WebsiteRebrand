Asked often04

The four most-asked.

Q · 01Is this for venture-backed companies only?+

No. The rubric is the same for bootstrapped, sponsored, and backed. The only filter is whether the numbers are live and the team can absorb a fortnightly read-out.

Q · 02What if a vital is "insufficient data"?+

It scores as Insuf. and goes into the ninety-day plan as the first instrumentation task. A red is a finding. An insuf. is a question that has not been asked.

Q · 03Will you sign an NDA before access?+

Yes. It is mutual and pre-printed and takes ninety seconds. Read-only access to the stack is part of intake.

Q · 04Can the read-out be presented to a board?+

Yes. The MRI is written for an operator first, but the document is paginated and worded to drop into a board pack. Boards are an explicit audience.

If your numbers are live but the next move is unclear, that's the MRI.
