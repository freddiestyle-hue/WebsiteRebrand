Design system/05 Templates
Section 05 · Composition reference

Six templates. No seventh.

Every page rivett ships is one of these. Home. Field note. Revenue MRI. Field-notes index. Diagnostic intake. 404. Three are built; three are anatomy-only and ship when needed.

T1 Home

Hero, modules, Tuesday list, audience, notes, dark close.

The single canonical home page. Stamp. Display. Italic dek. Three modules. The Tuesday list. Audience triad. Pull quote. Three field notes. Dark closer. Site footer.
