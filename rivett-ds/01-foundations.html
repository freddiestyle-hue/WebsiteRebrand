Section 01 · The atomic layer

Foundations. Tokens decide everything.

Colour, type, spacing, motion, grid, radii. The values you must not invent. Every component, pattern, and template downstream resolves to something on this page.

01 · 1 Colour

Paper, ink, a single green.

Two warm papers, one dark ink, one accent in two values, plus a tinted sage. That is the entire palette. No tertiaries, no semantic ramps, no gradient stops.

Ink is the dark surface. Ink-dim is italic serif on light. Ink-faint is the meta strip. Ink-soft is disabled and hairline-on-dark only. Never invent an ink-3 or ink-4.

Signal · functional reds and ambers, used sparingly

Signal · warn--signal-warn#B8651F · MRI bars only
Signal · halt--signal-halt#9A2B2B · halt / decline

No success colour. Success is the accent. No info colour. Info is ink-dim. The signal pair is for genuinely functional UI states only — Revenue MRI risk bars, halt buttons, error banners. Never for decoration.

The accent green is decorative — the dot, hover fills, badges. Never set body text or controls in --accent on light. For accent-coloured type, always use --accent-deep.

Pairing rules

  • Roman first clause, italic second clause — always.
  • Never bold + italic together. Italic is light by definition.
  • Mono is uppercase only above 14px. At 11px and 10.5px it is uppercase by token.
  • Never set body copy in serif. Newsreader is for the italic clause and pull quotes only.
  • Tabular figures (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums) for any column of numbers in a table or numbox.

01 · 4 Grid

1240 max, 12-column, 24-gap.

Every page sits in a 1240px max container with 56px side padding desktop, 24px mobile. The 12-column grid is referenced for editorial layouts and the section-scaffold split (240/1fr).

Section scaffold · the canonical 240/1fr split

Every spec page uses a 240px label rail + 1fr body. The label is mono, sticky, with a 56px section number underneath. The body is fluid up to its content max-width. Readable prose is constrained to --page-max-prose (720px). No prose runs the full 1240.

Breakpoints

  • ≤ 980px — section scaffold collapses to single column.
  • ≤ 880px — swatch and icon grids halve. Mark plate stacks.
  • ≤ 780px — page padding drops to 24px.
  • ≤ 680px — TOC drops to one column.
  • No tablet-specific breakpoint. Either it works at 768 or you fix the component.
01 · 5 Motion

Quiet transitions. One curve.

The brand barely animates. Hover crossfades the colour. Expanders rotate the marker. Page-level motion is reserved for the very rare full-bleed hero.

Colour swap 120ms

--dur-1 · link colour, button bg, border

Expand · transform 200ms

--dur-2 · FAQ marker, modal open

Page · fill 320ms

--dur-3 · MRI bar fill, very rare

Easing

--ease

cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.6, 0.2, 1) — quick start, soft settle. One curve covers everything in the system.

Reduced motion

Honoured everywhere. The tokens.css base reset zeroes animation and transition duration when prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is set. Components must not override this. Page-level scroll smoothing is also disabled in this mode.

01 · 6 Focus, z, elevation

Visible focus, flat by default.

Three rules. Focus is always 2px accent-deep on light or accent on dark, offset 2px. Elevation is forbidden except for modals. Z is layered in five steps and never improvised.

Focus ring

On light · --accent-deep
Focused button
On dark · --accent
Focused button

Z layers

  • --z-base : 1 — page content.
  • --z-sticky : 10 — sticky labels, sticky nav.
  • --z-overlay : 100 — image lightbox, MRI overlays.
  • --z-modal : 200 — diagnostic booking modal.
  • --z-toast : 300 — confirmation toasts (used very sparingly).

Elevation

There are exactly two non-zero shadows in the system. --shadow-1 is a 1px gravity press for cards on hover, almost imperceptible. --shadow-2 lifts the modal from the page. There is no card shadow, no nav shadow, no input shadow. Default elevation is zero.
