**TL;DR:** Rivett's pipeline is zero right now. The April list of warm conversations went cold. LinkedIn alone has not been enough. The plan for the next four weeks is engineering as a channel, the Vercel and Stripe playbook adapted to a one-operator practice. I am open-sourcing the AEO infrastructure I built for rivett.tech, shipping a free audit tool other operators can use, and publishing a Field Note every Friday about what got built that week. This is the post that starts the chain.

I will not bury the lead. Rivett's pipeline is zero this week.

The April list of warm conversations went quiet. None of them closed. None of them are still open. That is not a story about the leads, it is a story about the practice. A one-operator fractional growth consultancy with zero active conversations is not a healthy business, it is a project that has not figured out its demand-gen channel yet.

So this Field Note is the start of the rebuild.

## What Was Not Working

The previous theory was that LinkedIn would compound. Post enough, comment enough, and the warm intros would arrive. That worked for about six weeks earlier this year and then stopped working. The posts get views. The views do not convert into conversations.

The honest read: LinkedIn is necessary but not sufficient. The signal it produces is too thin to carry a fractional practice on its own. Operators see a good post and forget about it. They do not remember who wrote it next week when they need help.

What is missing is an artifact. A thing that exists in the world, that people can use without me being in the room, that signals "this operator builds working systems, not slides about systems."

## Engineering As A Channel

Vercel built itself by shipping Next.js. Stripe built itself by shipping good docs and the Stripe Press books. Linear ships product so opinionated that engineers tweet about each release. The pattern is the same. The product is the marketing. The building IS the channel.

For a fractional growth practice, the equivalent is not docs or books or hosted infrastructure. It is operator-shaped AI tooling, shipped publicly, useful to other operators, voiced for them, free.

So that is the bet for the next four weeks. Four artifacts. One Field Note per week about what I built that week. One LinkedIn long-form post chained to each Field Note. By the end of week four, Rivett will have shipped more public demand-gen than most fractional practices ship in a year, and every piece will chain into the next.

## The Plan

Four weeks. Four surfaces.

- Week one: open-source the AEO engine I just built for rivett.tech. Schema, llms.txt generation, validation. MIT licensed. github.com/freddiestyle-hue/aeo-engine.
- Week two: hosted audit at audit.rivett.tech. Paste a URL, get a scored AEO report. Free, no email required to see your score.
- Week three: email-gated fix kit download. Score is public, the actual paste-ready code is gated. Lead capture starts here.
- Week four (optional): Astro integration as an npm package. One command to make any Astro blog AI-readable.

Each week ends with a Field Note and a LinkedIn long-form. The build is the marketing. If you are reading this Field Note, you are seeing the first move.

## What Makes The Audit Tool Different

There are already AEO audit tools. HubSpot, Sona, ProductRank, LLMrefs. All of them target SEO marketers and brand managers who already have traffic. None of them target operators.

The hosted audit will be operator-voiced and open about its own constraints. The report will not say "improve your structured data implementation." It will say "ChatGPT cites your competitor when answering questions about your space. Here is why, and here is the code to fix it." Same checks under the hood, completely different feel.

The differentiation is two things. One, voice. Two, the fact that the entire engine is open source. The audit you got was literally generated by the code you can read.

## FAQ

**Why is the pipeline zero?**
Because the demand-gen theory was incomplete. LinkedIn was the only channel and it stopped producing warm conversations. Diagnosing that publicly is part of how the next theory gets stress-tested.

**Is the AEO engine going to be free forever?**
Yes. The repo is MIT licensed. The hosted audit at audit.rivett.tech will be free for any single URL audit. Lead capture is via the optional fix-kit download. There is no paywall on the score.

**Will the audit work on my non-Astro site?**
Yes. It runs on the rendered HTML, not the framework. The fix kit ZIP is tailored per detected stack though. Astro, Next.js, WordPress, and generic HTML are the launch targets. Other stacks land in the generic kit.

**What if no operators use the audit?**
That is the central bet of engineering-as-channel. The kill criterion is honest. If at ninety days there are zero retainer conversations sourced from the audit, the engine gets cut and I move to direct LinkedIn cadence plus ghost-writing for a high-DA newsletter. The two-tier kill gate is documented in the design doc.

**Are you hiring or looking for help?**
No. This is a one-operator practice by design. The capacity ceiling is three to four clients. The Visibility Engine is not an attempt to scale the team, it is an attempt to scale the demand surface so the existing capacity actually gets used.
